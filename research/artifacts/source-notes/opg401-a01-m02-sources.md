# Source-faithfulness note: m02 and the discharging route

Status: candidate_only. Retrieved 2026-09-06.
Repository base: 7da5e4f96aab1566b2110d82df377de03e618485.
No external theorem is a premise of the new pentagon calculation.

1. Xuding Zhu, author-hosted problem page:
https://www.math.nsysu.edu.tw/~zhu/open-problems/chic-k3free-planar.htm
The indexed primary-source text states the circular 20/7 conjecture
for triangle-free planar graphs of maximum degree three. This matches
the frozen contract after its finite/simple conventions. It separately
discusses fractional coloring. Those are not interchangeable parameters.
The historical page is not proof of current unresolved status.

2. Open Problem Garden, problem 401:
https://openproblemgarden.org/comment/reply/401
The retrieved problem text matches the same graph class and bound.
Its dodecahedron benchmark is source context, not a premise used by m02.
No novelty or exhaustive current-status finding is claimed.

3. D. W. Cranston and J. Li, Circular Flows in Planar Graphs:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.09833
https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1242513
The primary abstract gives circular 5/2-flow existence for
10-edge-connected planar graphs and circular 7/3-flow existence for
16-edge-connected planar graphs. The corresponding dual coloring
restrictions are much stronger girth restrictions than the present
girth-at-least-four contract. The maximum-degree-three assumption
does not supply either high edge-connectivity hypothesis in the dual.
Relation: possible discharging/modulo-orientation methodology, not a
theorem covering this root or the exact five-vertex boundary relation.

Only indexed primary text and abstracts were used; no PDF or full
paper was analyzed. The new calculations and critical consequences
are supplied explicitly in the candidate rather than attributed to
these sources. The missing root step is still reducibility covering
the unavoidable face family, with trusted mathematical verification.
